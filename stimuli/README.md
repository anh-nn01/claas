# 🖼️ Health Campaign Posters

This folder contains the **36 public health campaign posters** (visual stimuli) evaluated by participants in our dataset.

* Each poster corresponds to a designated communication strategy (`threatening`, `self-efficacy`, or `informational / neutral`).
* Poster filenames and their respective strategy labels are mapped in [`media_style_posters.csv`](../media_style_posters.csv).


To comply with the **University of Maryland (UMD) Institutional Review Board (IRB)** restrictions on hosting human health-related data on public repositories, we distribute our dataset under a **gated access policy**.

If you are interested in reproducing our results or utilizing the dataset for your research, please contact **[Professor Tracy Zeeger](https://sph.umd.edu/people/tracy-zeeger)** at **[tzeeger@umd.edu](mailto:tzeeger@umd.edu)** to request access.
